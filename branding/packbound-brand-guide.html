The Signature Mark

The real pin, not an abstraction

Each marker follows the classic map-pin construction people already trust: a solid colour teardrop, a white ring, and a darker centre dot in the same hue. Nothing invented, nothing to learn.

Three of them, one composition: coral leads, centred and closest. Teal and amber sit level behind it, side by side — equal packmates, not a queue. Simple shapes, three colours, no extra ornament — and now properly centred with even breathing room on every side.

In-App Marker Colours

A separate palette for real map pins

The logo's coral/teal/amber is fixed brand identity — it doesn't change. But the live map markers each pack member actually sees are limited to the platform's built-in marker hues: Red, Orange, Yellow, Cyan, Blue, Magenta, Rose. That's a technical constraint (default map SDK marker icons only tint to these seven), not a brand choice, so it's documented separately here rather than folded into the palette above.

These are softened to match what actually renders on the map rather than pure digital hues. Red is anchored to Google's own sampled pointer colour (#EA4335); the other six are the same tone rotated round the colour wheel, since Google doesn't publish exact values for the rest — worth a quick eyedropper check against a real device screenshot before this is final. Red is reserved for whoever is leading, echoing the logo's coral lead marker; the other six assign to packmates in the order shown, cycling if a group runs past seven people.
