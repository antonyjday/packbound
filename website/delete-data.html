Delete your data

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This page covers data deletion for Packbound, the app published on Google Play. Packbound has no account system - sign-in is anonymous, so most of your data is already deleted automatically without you having to ask. This page explains exactly what that covers, what's left over, and how to get that removed too.

How to request deletion

Because sign-in is anonymous (no email, phone number, or password), there's no login we can use to look up your data for you. Instead:

  1. Email support@packbound.net from any address, with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include the display name(s) you used in the app and roughly when you last used it, so we can find the right record(s) - this is the only identifying information Packbound ever has about you.
  3. We'll delete the record and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

What's deleted automatically (no request needed)

  • Live location, quick messages, and voice clips - deleted immediately the moment a trip ends, whether it's ended manually, goes inactive, or hits its 24-hour cap.
  • Trip records (trip name and who was in it) - kept for 30 days after the trip ends in case you want to look back on it, then permanently deleted.

What's kept until you request deletion

  • Your device's profile record - the display name you last chose and when you last opened the app - persists after a trip ends, since it isn't tied to any one trip. It's kept indefinitely until you email us as above, at which point it's permanently deleted.
  • Push notification token - deleted at the same time as the profile record above, since it's stored alongside it.

For the full picture of what Packbound collects and why, see the privacy policy.
