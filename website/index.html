Features

Everything a group needs to actually stay together

Packbound isn't a general-purpose tracker - it's built around the one thing a travel group needs: knowing where everyone is, for exactly as long as the trip lasts.

🔒

No account needed

Sign in with just a display name. No email, no phone number, no password to lose.

🔑

Create or join a trip

Start a trip and invite people by a 6-character code, a QR code, or a direct link - whatever's easiest to share in the moment.

📍

Live map

Everyone's position, heading, and speed on one shared map, with a clear signal status - live, weak, or lost - per person.

📱

Background sharing

Keeps reporting your position while the app is minimized, with a one-tap toggle whenever you want to pause it.

🚘

Trip type, mode-aware

Car, Train, Bicycle, or Walk - tunes the map's zoom, the quick-message presets, and the actual travel mode used for routes and ETAs.

🧭

Shared route & turn-by-turn

The trip owner sets a start point, destination, and stops. Every member gets their own live ETA - distance, duration, and estimated arrival time - plus turn-by-turn directions.

⌛

Trips can't run forever

Trips auto-expire after 24 hours (the owner can extend) or after 10 hours of inactivity, with warnings as the deadline approaches.

💬

Quick messages & push-to-talk

One-tap preset messages relevant to your trip type - "Need fuel", "Missed the train" - or a tap-to-record voice clip, sent as an alert plus a push notification.

🔋

Low battery & lost-signal alerts

The group is notified if your battery gets critically low, or if your signal goes quiet - or if you've simply arrived.

How it works

From "let's go" to "we made it" in four steps

Start a trip

Pick a display name, name the trip, and choose how you're travelling.

Bring your pack

Share the invite code, a QR code, or a link - whoever joins shows up on the map instantly.

Share the route

The owner sets a destination and any stops; everyone gets their own live ETA and turn-by-turn directions.

Trip ends, data goes

End it manually, let it go quiet, or hit the 24h cap - either way, location history is wiped immediately.

Privacy

Built to know as little about you as possible

Location sharing only works if people trust it. Packbound is built so there's as little to trust with as possible - here's exactly what happens to your data, not just a promise.

👤No account, no personal details

Sign-in is anonymous - Packbound never asks for an email address, phone number, or password. The only thing you provide is a display name, which is only ever visible to people in your own trip.

📡Location is shared, not broadcast

Your live position is only ever visible to the other members of the specific trip you're on - never to other Packbound users, and never to us for any purpose beyond making the app work.

⏳Deleted the moment the trip ends

Whether the owner ends the trip, it goes quiet, or it hits the 24-hour hard cap, location history is deleted immediately - not "eventually," not "on the next cleanup run."

🗑️Trip details don't linger either

Basic trip info (its name, who was in it) sticks around for 30 days after a trip ends, in case you want to look back - then it's permanently purged too. Nothing is kept indefinitely.

🚫No ads, no analytics, nothing sold

There's no advertising SDK, no analytics or tracking library, and no third party this data is ever sold or handed to. The app simply doesn't include that code.

📖Open about all of it

Packbound's source code is public on GitHub. Rather than take our word for any of this, you can read the actual code that handles your location data yourself.

Packbound runs on Firebase (Google Cloud) for accounts, live sync, and push notifications, and the Google Maps Platform for maps and routing - both process data only as needed to run the app. See the full privacy policy for details, or browse the source yourself.
